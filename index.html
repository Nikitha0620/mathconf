About Us

Conference & Host Institutions

ICCMI-2026: International Conference on Contemporary Mathematics and Innovations

The International Conference on Contemporary Mathematics and Innovations (ICCMI-2026) brings together leading mathematicians, researchers, and industry experts to explore cutting-edge developments across pure, applied, and computational mathematics. The conference highlights the role of mathematical modelling, analysis, and modern computational techniques in driving innovation across multidisciplinary domains. ICCMI-2026 provides a dynamic platform for exchanging ideas, presenting high- quality research, and international collaborations.

Thiagarajar College of Engineering

Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE), established in 1957 by the philanthropist Karumuttu Thiagarajan Chettiar, is a premier government-aided autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University and approved by AICTE. Accredited with NAAC A++ (3.56 out of 4) and with several programmes accredited by NBA, TCE offers UG, PG, and Ph.D. programmes supported by advanced laboratories, strong industry collaborations, and innovative CDIO- and OBE-based teaching frameworks.

Visit Website

Thiagarajar College (Autonomous)

Thiagarajar College (TC), was founded on 7th July 1949 by Kalaithanthai Karumuttu Thiagarajan Chettiar, a great philanthropist and visionary of 20th century. The college which started with only three undergraduate programmes, has now significantly expanded its academic excellence by offering 31 UG, 15 PG and 10 Ph.D. programmes. Recognizing the high standards of the institution in the accreditation cycles, NAAC has extended the accreditation validity period from 5 years to 7 years. The college has also demonstrated consistent improvement in NIRF rankings scaling up from 44th in 2018 to 20th in 2025.

Organizing Mathematics Departments
TCE - Department of Mathematics

The Department was established in 1957. The Department of Mathematics equips engineering students with essential mathematical skills and knowledge through a diverse range of courses tailored to different engineering programs. The Department of Mathematics strives to maintain high standards of teaching to the students with potential faculty members.

TC - Department of Mathematics

The department of Mathematics was established in the year 1949 and upgraded as a postgraduate department in 1959. The department of Mathematics comprises 22 highly qualified and dedicated faculty members specialized in diverse areas such as Graph theory, Analysis, Algorithmic Graph Theory, Differential Equations and Topology.
