About Us

Conference & Host Institutions

ICCMI-2026: International Conference on Contemporary Mathematics and Innovations

The International Conference on Contemporary Mathematics and Innovations (ICCMI-2026) brings together leading mathematicians, researchers, and industry experts to explore cutting-edge developments across pure, applied, and computational mathematics. The conference highlights the role of mathematical modelling, analysis, and modern computational techniques in driving innovation across multidisciplinary domains. ICCMI-2026 provides a dynamic platform for exchanging ideas, presenting high-quality research, and international collaborations.

Thiagarajar College of Engineering

Established in 1957 by philanthropist Karumuttu Thiagarajan Chettiar, TCE is a premier government-aided autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University and approved by AICTE. Accredited with NAAC A++ (3.56/4) and NBA-accredited programmes, TCE offers UG, PG, and Ph.D. programmes with advanced laboratories, strong industry collaborations, and innovative CDIO- and OBE-based teaching frameworks.

Visit Website

Thiagarajar College (Autonomous)

Founded on 7th July 1949 by Kalaithanthai Karumuttu Thiagarajan Chettiar, TA has expanded from three undergraduate programmes to 31 UG, 15 PG, and 10 Ph.D. programmes. NAAC has extended accreditation validity from 5 to 7 years, recognizing exceptional standards. The college demonstrates consistent NIRF ranking improvement: 44th (2018) to 20th (2025).
