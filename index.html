About TCE

Thiagarajar College of Engineering

Thiagarajar College of Engineering (TCE), established in 1957 by the philanthropist Karumuttu Thiagarajan Chettiar, is a premier government-aided autonomous institution affiliated to Anna University and approved by AICTE. Accredited with NAAC A++ (3.56 out of 4) and with several programmes accredited by NBA, TCE offers UG, PG, and Ph.D. programmes supported by advanced laboratories, strong industry collaborations, and innovative CDIO- and OBE-based teaching frameworks

About TA

Thiagarajar College of Arts and Science

Thiagarajar College (TA), was founded on 7th July 1949 by Kalaithanthai Karumuttu Thiagarajan Chettiar, a great philanthropist and visionary of 20th century. The college which started with only three undergraduate programmes, has now significantly expanded its academic excellence by offering 31 UG, 15 PG and 10 Ph.D. programmes. Recognizing the high standards of the institution in the accreditation cycles, NAAC has extended the accreditation validity period from 5 years to 7 years. The college has also demonstrated consistent improvement in NIRF rankings scaling up from 44th in 2018 to 20th in 2025.

About Conference

About Conference

The International Conference on Contemporary Mathematics and Innovations (ICCMI-2026) brings together leading mathematicians, researchers, and industry experts to explore cutting-edge developments across pure, applied, and computational mathematics. The conference highlights the role of mathematical modelling, analysis, and modern computational techniques in driving innovation across multidisciplinary domains. ICCMI-2026 provides a dynamic platform for exchanging ideas, presenting high-quality research, and international collaborations.

Theme(s) of the conference

  • Algebra and Number Theory
  • Computational Fluid Dynamics
  • Computational Geometry
  • Differential Equations and Dynamical systems
  • Discrete Mathematics and Cryptography
  • Graph theory and network science
  • Machine Learning and Deep Learning
  • Mathematical Biology and Bioinformatics
  • Mathematical Modeling
  • Numerical analysis
  • Optimization Techniques
  • Stochastic modeling
  • Topology and Functional Analysis

Scope of the conference

  • IC-STEEH aims to promote interdisciplinary innovation at the intersection of science and technology to drive greener and sustainable solutions, and to enhance societal well-being.

Call for Papers

The participants are requested to submit the extended abstracts including manuscript title, author(s), affiliation(s) and contact details, with a clear indication of research work, methodology, major results and conclusion. The committee will evaluate the abstracts and will decide whether the paper will be presented as oral or poster, also considering the preference of the authors.

Publications

The registered and successfully presented papers in the conference will be considered for publication in the Scopus Indexed International journals after the peer review process.

Publication Scope under Agriculture discipline

The selected manuscripts after the presentation in the conference will be published as a special issue in Plant Science Today (eISSN: 2348-1900), a Scopus-indexed journal (NAAS: 6.7). Only the manuscripts that successfully pass the peer-review process will be included in the special issue. After submission of the revised manuscript, the publication process typically takes about 2–3 months. Acceptance emails will be sent to the authors directly by the journal. The Authors are required to pay the following Article Processing Charges (APC): USD 160 for faculty and USD 128 for students from India, USD 192 for authors from other countries, plus 18% GST.
